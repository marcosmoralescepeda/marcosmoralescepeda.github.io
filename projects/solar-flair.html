Starting with the user

We ran surveys, interviews and observations with campers, hikers and homeowners to find out what they actually needed from a flashlight.

What users needed

  • Reliable light in places without power
  • Long battery life
  • Durable enough for outdoor use
  • Easy to recharge
  • Eco-friendly
  • Affordable

Who we designed for

  • Outdoor enthusiasts, ages 18 to 35
  • Active lifestyle, often off the grid
  • Value reliability and sustainability
"I need a flashlight that works when I need it most, even when I'm off the grid."Survey participant

How we built it

We went through several prototypes and used user feedback to improve performance, durability and ease of use at each step.

  1. Research

    Surveys and interviews to define the real needs.

  2. Concepts

    Sketches, then design matrices to pick the best idea.

  3. CAD model

    3D model and a dimensioned technical drawing.

  4. Print & build

    Body printed in PETG, then assembled with the panel, battery and LED.

  5. Test

    Lab-style tests plus a usability test with real users.
